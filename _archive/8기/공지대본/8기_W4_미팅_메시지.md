## ③ 진행 대본 (60분 · 발표 모드 7장 기준)

**[시작 전]** 발표 모드 열기 · 녹화 시작 · 마지막 슬라이드 받아적기 칸 확인

**[1. 오프닝 · 5분]**
"Hi everyone! Welcome to our last meeting. Four weeks went by fast, right?
Tonight is a celebration. We'll run four things: your sentences one last time, word bingo, one big roleplay, and then we wrap up your script. Same rule as always, this is the place to make mistakes. Last chance to use it!"

**[2. 내 문장 발표 · 15분]**
"Let's go around. Four things, short and sweet.
One, how was your week, one line.
Two, read out one sentence you wrote this week.
Three, tell us ONE expression from these four weeks that became truly yours. When did you actually use it?
And four, everyone gives one comment. Who wants to start?"

**[3. 칠판 + 빙고 · 10분]**
"Here's everything, sixty expressions in four weeks. Look at the board and mark the ones that don't come out of your mouth easily. Those are your bingo targets tonight.
Now, bingo rules: when you actually SAY an expression in a real sentence about YOUR situation, tap the square. One line or eight squares, bingo!"

**[4. 종합 롤플레잉 · 15분]**
"We're going to run one imaginary project together, from kickoff to crisis to wrap-up.
Week 1 words for status updates, Week 2 for numbers, Week 3 when things go wrong, Week 4 to sell your results. I'll set each scene, you jump in. Bingo board is live!"
(막히면 칠판에서 힌트, 표현 나올 때마다 빙고판 마크)

**[5. 60개 스윕 · 8분]**
"Quick sweep, Korean to English. Don't aim for perfect, just flag the ones that stall with the bookmark. Those go into your final review."

**[6. 마무리 · 7분]**
- 각자 "내 것이 된 표현 3개" → 받아적기 칸에 기록
