## 1. 오프닝 (5분)

"Hi everyone! Welcome to our very last meeting. Can you believe it? Four weeks, just like that.

Tonight is a celebration, not a class. Four things: your sentences one last time, then presentations, a quiz game, and we wrap up your script.

Same rule as week one: this is the place to make mistakes. Tonight is your last chance to use it, so use it well!"

(가볍게 근황 체크) "Quick check-in first. How was your week, everyone? One word is enough!"

## 2. 내 문장 발표 · 마지막 라운드 (12분)

"Let's go around, one by one. Three things tonight.

One, read out one sentence you wrote this week.
Two, tell us ONE expression from these four weeks that became truly yours. Not the hardest one, the one you actually USED. When did you use it?
And three, everyone else gives one comment.

Who wants to start?"

- [ ] 슬라이드의 "이번 주 여러분이 쓴 문장" 박스 보면서 진행. 막히는 멤버에겐 "이 문장 누가 쓰셨죠?" 로 시작
- [ ] "내 것이 된 표현"은 받아적기. 수료식 콘텐츠 재료가 됨
- 칭찬은 구체적으로: "그 상황에서 그 단어를 골랐다는 게 좋아요" 식

## 3. 발표 시간 (30분)

"Now, the main event. {발표자}, the floor is yours!"

- [ ] 발표 전, 청중에게: "Everyone, two missions while you listen. Write down any words you didn't catch, and prepare ONE question, like a real meeting. Use this month's expressions if you can!"
- [ ] 발표 후 순서: ① 버디의 구체적 칭찬 하나 ("Your opening was really clear, especially...") ② 질문 라운드 ③ 동료 코멘트

발표 마무리 멘트:
"That was fantastic. Four weeks ago, would you have imagined giving this presentation in English? That's the distance you've covered."

## 4. 랜덤 단어 퀴즈 (8분)

"Okay, speed round! I'll share my screen. Random five words, Korean meaning first. Shout out the English, first one takes it!"

- [ ] 홈의 [랜덤 단어 테스트] 버튼 화면 공유로 진행, 2~3판
- [ ] 막히면 힌트 버튼 (단어 하나씩 열림) 활용
- "Don't worry about the ones you missed. The final test opens tomorrow, that's your review list!"

## 5. 수료 마무리 (5분)

"Last slide. Three things before we say goodbye.

First, your homework tonight is simple: pick three expressions that became yours. Type them in, I'm writing them down.

Second, your script. Everything you wrote for four weeks is in MY SCRIPTS. Tomorrow after the final test, you also get the vocab book PDF. It has all sixty words, my notes on each one, and even a paper test at the back. That book is yours forever.

Third, the finish line: eighteen days plus the final test. No deadline, late days still count. See you at the finish line!"
