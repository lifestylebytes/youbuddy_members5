# Slide 1 · 오프닝 (오늘 순서)

"Hi Juhye, hi Dohyun! Good to see you both again.

Quick recap of where we are: Week 1 we built up your everyday work expressions, Week 2 we played with light workplace humor, and today, Week 3, is one of the most practical sessions for a foreign company: **cushion language**.

Here's the idea. In Korean, you show respect through 존댓말, the grammar itself. English has no 존댓말. So instead, we soften everything through **word choice** and **sentence patterns**. The exact same message can sound either pushy or polite, depending on the cushion you put around it.

So today isn't about reading a list. My goal is simple: by the end, when I throw a blunt sentence at you, you can **soften it on the spot, out loud**. That reflex is the whole point.

One housekeeping note: Mason couldn't join live, so I'll share the recording afterward. Alright, let's get into it."

(서보경님 녹화 안내 한 줄. 바로 다음으로.)

# Slide 2 · 미니 강연: "영어엔 존댓말이 없어요"

"Let me show you why this matters with one tiny example.

If you say, *'Can you help me with this?'* it's not wrong, but to a native ear it can feel a little abrupt, a little demanding. Watch what a small cushion does:

❌ *'Can you help me with this?'*
✅ *'I was wondering if you could help me with this when you have a moment.'*

It's longer, yes. But that length is exactly what signals politeness in English. There's no 존댓말 ending to lean on, so the cushion does that job.

And here's the good news: this is not about advanced grammar. These are **reusable patterns**. Native speakers don't invent a new polite sentence every time. They keep a handful of cushion structures and just drop the content in. Learn the structure, and you sound professional automatically."

(❌ vs ✅ 한 쌍 화면에 띄우고 천천히 읽어주기. 길이 차이를 직접 느끼게.)

---

# Slide 3 · 칠판: 쿠션어 20선 · 4개 서랍

"On the board are twenty expressions, grouped into four 'drawers.' You don't need to memorize all twenty today. Just notice which drawer feels weakest for you, and we'll drill that one.

**Drawer 1 · Softening a request:**
*I was wondering if… / Would it be possible to… / Could you help me with… / Do you think you could… / When you have a moment…*

**Drawer 2 · Giving an opinion or disagreeing:**
*From my perspective… / I might be wrong, but… / It seems like… / I'm not entirely sure, but… / One thing to consider is…*

**Drawer 3 · Raising a problem, delay, or risk:**
*There might be a delay in… / We may need to revisit this. / This could potentially affect… / I just want to flag that… / Just to make sure we're aligned…*

**Drawer 4 · Modern softeners to lower the tone:**
*Just to clarify… / If that makes sense. / Let me know your thoughts. / I'd appreciate it if… / No rush at all.*

So, quick gut check from each of you: which drawer do you reach for the least at work right now?"

(각자 약한 서랍 1개씩 말하게 → 오늘 집중 카테고리 정함.)

# Slide 4 · 드릴 진행 방식 (❌ → ✅)

"Here's how the drill works, and it's a little game.

I throw a blunt, direct sentence at you, like *'This won't work.'* Your job is to **soften it out loud, right away**, before you peek at the answer. Then we open the model answer, compare, and you say it one more time. Finally, you turn it into a sentence from your own real job.

The model answers and the key phrase are blurred on screen, so try first, then reveal. And there's a phrase bank at the bottom of each slide, so you can grab a cushion from there and build your own version. Whoever softens it fastest wins the round. Ready?"

(게임처럼 가볍게. "직설 폭탄 → 바로 부드럽게 → 정답 열기 → 내 상황" 4단계 강조.)

# Slide 5 · 드릴 1 / 4 · 🙏 요청을 부드럽게

진행: 운영자가 ❌만 읽어주고, 멤버가 쿠션어로 바꿔 말함 → "👀 모범답안 보기"로 ✅ 확인 → 한 번 더.

"Drawer one, requests. I'll read the blunt version. You soften it."

1. ❌ "Can I change my vacation dates?"
   > (멤버 시도 → 공개) ✅ *"I was wondering if it'd be possible to adjust my vacation dates?"*
   > "Notice the double cushion: *I was wondering if* plus *would it be possible*. Very safe for a sensitive ask."

2. ❌ "Can you show me how to use this?"
   > ✅ *"Could you help me with this when you have a moment?"*
   > "*Could* over *can*, and *when you have a moment* respects their time."

3. ❌ "We need to postpone the meeting."
   > ✅ *"Would it be possible to reschedule our meeting to next week?"*
   > "*Would it be possible* turns a one-way announcement into a request."

> "Now each of you: take one of these and rebuild it with a real situation from your own week. Use the phrase bank at the bottom if you get stuck."

---

# Slide 6 · 드릴 2 / 4 · 💬 의견·반대·조정

"Drawer two, the meeting drawer. This is where blunt disagreement gets people defensive. Soften it."

1. ❌ "I think B is better."
   > ✅ *"From my perspective, Option B might give us more flexibility."*
2. ❌ "That's not going to work."
   > ✅ *"I might be wrong, but I think the client doesn't support that."*
   > "*I might be wrong, but* is the strongest cushion you have for disagreeing. It protects everyone's face."
3. ❌ "Our strategy isn't working."
   > ✅ *"It seems like our numbers have dropped. Maybe we test a new angle?"*
4. ❌ "This timeline is unrealistic."
   > ✅ *"One thing to consider is whether we'll have enough time for QA."*
   > "*One thing to consider* reframes pushback as an extra angle, not a 'no.'"

> "Juhye, this drawer is gold for HR and stakeholder meetings. Dohyun, same for pushing back on an ops plan without sounding difficult. Each of you, one sentence from your real work."

---

# Slide 7 · 드릴 3 / 4 · 🚩 일정·문제·리스크

"Drawer three, bad news. The skill here is flagging a problem without causing panic or blame."

1. ❌ "The vendor is late. We'll miss the deadline."
   > ✅ *"There might be a delay in the delivery. Should we adjust the timeline?"*
2. ❌ "That approach was wrong."
   > ✅ *"We may need to revisit the approach we discussed last week."*
   > "*Revisit* is neutral. Nobody gets blamed, you just reopen it."
3. ❌ "You forgot the IP clause."
   > ✅ *"I just want to flag that I didn't see the IP clause, am I missing it?"*
   > "*I just want to flag that* is probably the most-used phrase in modern workplaces. It sounds like sharing, not accusing."

> "Each of you, think of a real risk you'd have to raise this month, and flag it with a cushion."

---

# Slide 8 · 드릴 4 / 4 · 🧸 톤 낮추는 완충

"Drawer four, the email and Slack closers. Small softeners that change the whole tone."

1. ❌ "I don't understand what you mean."
   > ✅ *"Just to clarify, do you mean animations or graphics?"*
2. ❌ "Send me the file by EOD."
   > ✅ *"I'd appreciate it if you could send the file by EOD. Thanks so much!"*
3. ❌ "Can you review this?"
   > ✅ *"When you get a chance, could you take a look? No rush at all."*
   > "*No rush at all* is everywhere in modern messaging. It signals you respect their priorities."

> "These are the ones you'll use ten times a day in Slack. Pick your favorite two and say them with your own request."
