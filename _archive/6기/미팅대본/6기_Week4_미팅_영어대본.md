# 유비챌 6기 · Week 4 (마지막) 미팅 영어 대본

진행자(Gaby)용. Week 4 = **4주 전체 어휘 랩업 + 수료**. 발표 모드 덱(m4) 슬라이드 흐름 그대로.
톤: 시험이 아니라 **축하 + 마무리**. 참석: 정주혜(Juhye/Hera) · 이도현(Dohyun/Andy) · Brad · 서보경(Mason, 녹화).
( ) 안은 한국어 진행 메모. 발표 피드백은 이번엔 없음.

---

# Slide 1 · 오프닝 (마지막 인사 + 순서)

"Hi everyone, and welcome to our very last session. It's a little bittersweet, honestly.

Four weeks ago, a lot of these expressions were just words on a screen. Today is not a test. Today is the day we prove, out loud, that they've actually become yours.

Here's the plan, and it's a fun one: we'll do a quick lap through all sixty expressions, play a word bingo, run one big roleplay that folds four weeks into a single meeting, do a fast review, and then wrap up with a few words for each of you.

One note: Mason couldn't join live, so I'll share the recording afterward. Alright, let's finish strong."

(가볍고 따뜻하게. "시험 아님, 증명하는 날" 프레이밍.)

---

# Slide 2 · 4주 칠판 (60개 훑기)

"First, a quick lap. Here are all sixty expressions from the four weeks, grouped by week.

We're not re-memorizing everything. Just skim with me, and mentally flag the ones that still don't come out instantly. Those are the ones we'll aim for in the bingo and the roleplay.

Week 1 was setup, Week 2 communication, Week 3 problem-solving, Week 4 execution. Take ten seconds per week. Which ones still feel shaky?"

(주차별로 훑으며, 각자 "아직 입에서 안 나오는 것" 눈도장 찍게. 오늘 노릴 단어.)

---

# Slide 3 · 단어 빙고

"Now the fun part, word bingo. On screen is a grid of expressions.

Here's the rule: whenever you use one of these words in a real sentence, out loud, during our conversation, we tap that cell to mark it. Not just reading it, an actual sentence from your own work. First to a line, across, down, or diagonal, or eight cells total, wins.

So the game is really just: keep talking, and keep sliding these expressions in naturally. Ready? Let's start marking as we go into the roleplay."

(칠판 그리드에서, 실제 문장으로 쓰면 그 칸 탭해서 마크. 한 줄 또는 8칸이면 빙고. 다음 롤플레잉하면서 같이 마크.)

---

# Slide 4 · 종합 롤플레잉 (한 회의에 4주 단어 녹이기)

"Let's run one big roleplay. We'll act out a single project meeting, and ride the natural arc: it kicks off, a problem hits, we solve it, and we wrap. If you follow that flow, all four weeks of vocabulary show up on their own.

I'll set the scene, you jump in. Use the board for hints, and mark your bingo as you go.

🟢 **Kickoff:** *pull in* the right people, *level-set* on the goal, decide who *runs point*, get a *ballpark*.
Juhye, you start us off, kick off the meeting.

🟡 **Discussion:** *weigh in*, *hash out* the details, do we *see eye to eye*? *flag* any concern.
Brad, jump in with a different opinion here.

🔴 **A problem hits:** what's the *root cause*? Let's *get to the bottom of* it, are we *cutting corners*, did we *hit a wall*?
Andy, you raise the problem.

🟣 **Wrap up:** do we *greenlight* it, *ship it*, *tie up loose ends*, and will it *move the needle*?
Let's land the plane together."

(운영자가 장면만 던지고 멤버가 받기. 막히면 칠판 힌트. 단어 쓸 때마다 빙고 마크. 한 바퀴 돌면 역할 바꿔 짧게 한 번 더.)

---

# Slide 5 · 60개 스윕 (빠른 복습)

"Lightning round to close out the words. I'll give you the Korean, you fire back the English. We'll focus on the ones you flagged as shaky earlier.

If you blank, just say 'pass', we mark it and move on. No pressure, this is just to feel how far you've come."

(워크시트로 한국어 → 영어 즉답. 막힌 것 위주. 🔖 표시.)

---

# Slide 6 · 수료 마무리 (한 사람씩 + 감사)

"Okay, let's wrap up our four weeks together.

Two quick things from each of you: tell me the three expressions that truly became yours, and one place you'll actually use them next week."

(각자 말하게. 받아적기.)

**그다음, 한 사람씩 '4주 전 → 지금' 한 줄 (미리 적어둔 것):**

> "Juhye, four weeks ago you said you wanted these to feel natural, not memorized. Watching you *level-set* and *flag* things today, in your own HR situations, that shift already happened. Really proud of you."

> "Andy, early on the guide roleplays would stall. Today you handled the whole problem, backup plan and all, in English. That's a huge jump."

> "Brad, ____ (4주 전 대비 관찰한 성장 한 줄)."

> "And Mason, I'll say this on the recording too, thank you for staying with us across the time difference."

(마무리 멘트)
"Here's my one wish. I don't need you to remember all sixty perfectly. I just need one of these to pop out, on its own, in a real meeting next week. That's the whole goal.

This isn't really the end. Your word book PDF and the review are always there, so keep them close. Thank you all for showing up, week after week. It genuinely meant a lot. 🧡"

(수료증·단어집 PDF·다음 스텝 안내. 두 분 다 끝까지 와준 것에 진심 감사. 받아적기 저장.)
