# 유비챌 6기 · Week 3 라이브 연습 진행 대본 (meeting-conductor-prototype)

페이지: `https://youbuddy.co.kr/meeting-conductor-prototype.html`
주제: **상황 롤플레이 × Day 1~15 단어 풀.** 운영자(Gaby)가 영어로 진행, ( ) 안은 한국어 메모.
참석: 정주혜(Juhye) · 이도현(Dohyun) · (서보경 녹화). 상황 3은 도현님 전용 롤플레잉.

> 페이지 쓰는 법: 헤더에 참석자 **이름 입력** → 상황 탭 선택 → **🎲 누가 시작?** 로 시작 사람 뽑기 → 카드의 💡 추천 표현 + 아래 **단어 풀**(Day 11~15 필터) 참고하며 대화. 막히면 단어 카드 펼쳐 예문 확인, 🔖 북마크.

---

# 0 · 오프닝 (페이지 열고)

"Alright, today we're doing something different. No slides, just real situations. I'll put a scenario on screen, we spin to see who starts, and we act it out in English, slipping in this week's words naturally.

Don't aim for perfect. Aim for **keeping the conversation going**. If you blank on a word, glance at the pool at the bottom, grab one, and keep talking. The screen shows a few suggested expressions for each situation, but you're free to pull from the whole pool.

Let me type your names in first... okay. Let's start with situation one."

(헤더에 이름 입력 → 상황 1 탭.)

---

# 1 · 🚨 갑자기 터진 장애 대응 회의 (5~7분, 2~3명)

추천 표현: **Root cause · Band-aid solution · Get to the bottom of**

"Here's the setup: your service just went down. The team huddles fast. The question on the table is, do we slap on a quick **band-aid solution** to stop the bleeding right now, or do we **get to the bottom of** the **root cause** first? Someone also needs to **run point** so it's not chaos.

Let's spin, who starts?

(🎲 누가 시작 → 시작 사람에게)
You're kicking off the incident call. Share what you're seeing in one or two lines, then steer us: band-aid now, or root cause first? And decide who runs point."

(진행 힌트: 상황 공유 → 임시방편 vs 근본 원인 → 누가 run point → 다음 액션.)
(중간 코칭) "Good, can you push it one level deeper? Don't just say 'fix it,' tell us whether that's a band-aid or the actual root cause."

---

# 2 · 🧭 할 일 산더미 · 우선순위 정리 1:1 (5~7분, 2명)

추천 표현: **Spin your wheels · In the weeds · Move the needle · Kick the can down the road**

"Now a one-on-one. Everyone's **spinning their wheels**, too much on the plate, lots of motion, no progress. You're sitting down with your manager to sort it out. The trap here is getting **in the weeds** on small stuff. Your job is to lead with what actually **moves the needle**, and be honest about what you'll **kick the can down the road** on.

Let's spin for who plays the member and who plays the manager.

(🎲 → 역할 배정)
Member, you start: walk your manager through where you're spinning your wheels, then propose what to drop and what to prioritize by impact."

(진행 힌트: 현황 공유 → 헛바퀴 지점 → 미룰 것 / 할 것 → 임팩트 기준 합의.)
(코칭) "Nice. Manager, push back a little, ask 'is that really moving the needle, or is it in the weeds?'"

---

# 3 · 🏝️ 도현님 · 투어 가이드 팀 회의 (롤플레잉, 7~10분)

추천 표현: **De-risk · Back to the drawing board · Get to the bottom of · Band-aid solution · Move the needle · Hit a wall**

진행: 운영자 = 본사 매니저. 도현님 = 가이드. 매니저가 시키호르(Siquijor) 새 코스 도입에 현실적 우려를 **즉석에서 던지고**, 도현님은 본인 경험으로 차분히 답 + 막히면 백업 플랜·대안. (질문은 화면에 없음. 아래를 운영자가 영어로 던짐.)

"Dohyun, let's roleplay. I'm a head-office manager, and you're the guide. We're thinking about rolling out a new course, **Siquijor**, and honestly I have a few concerns. I'm going to throw them at you one by one. Answer me from your real experience, and where it gets tricky, give me a backup plan. Ready?"

### 매니저가 던질 질문 5개 (영어, 하나씩 · 답 듣고 다음으로)

1. **이동 시간 / 동선**
   "My first worry is the travel time. Siquijor is a long round trip, and a big chunk of the day is just getting there and back. How do you keep guests happy when so much of the tour is travel? Walk me through a realistic timeline."

2. **컴플레인 · 응급 대응**
   "Things go wrong on the water and on the road, a guest gets seasick, someone's late, a minor injury. What's your plan when something actually goes wrong out there? I don't want a **band-aid solution**, I want to know you can handle it on the spot."

3. **업셀링 vs 컴플레인 줄타기**
   "We want guides to upsell, snorkeling add-ons, lunch upgrades, extra stops. But push too hard and we get complaints. How do you walk that line so it **moves the needle** on revenue without annoying people?"

4. **현지 가이드·크루 통제**
   "You won't be doing this alone, you'll rely on local crews. If a local guide goes off-script or the boat crew isn't reliable, how do you keep control and keep the experience consistent?"

5. **식당·현지 업체 문제**
   "Last one, the restaurants. If a partner restaurant is slow, dirty, or overbooked on the day, what's your backup? At what point do we say this course just isn't working and go **back to the drawing board**?"

(마무리) "Okay, good. So if you had to **de-risk** this whole thing before we launch, what are the top two changes you'd make? Give me your honest call."

(코칭 메모: 도현님이 막혀서 **hit a wall** 하면, "Take a breath, give me a backup plan, even a rough one." 으로 풀어주기. 압박이 목적이 아니라 표현 끌어내기.)

---

# 4 · 🎯 자유 · Day 1~15 전체 mix (2~3분)

추천: 단어 풀 전체에서 자유롭게.

"Last one is free. Each of you, pick a real business scenario you run into often, set it up in one line, and act it out for two or three minutes. Just naturally slip in one to three words from the pool. Pull up the Day 11 to 15 filter if you want today's words."

(🎲 또는 그냥 한 명씩. 본인 상황 1줄 → 단어 1~3개 픽 → 자유 대화.)

---

# 5 · 마무리 (2분)

"Let's close. Two quick things from each of you: which word came out the most naturally today, and which one still felt forced? The forced one is exactly what to drill this week.

(받아적기) Homework: use one of today's words for real this week, in a message or a meeting, and tell me how it landed next time. Great work, both of you."
