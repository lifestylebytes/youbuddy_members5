# Slide 2 · 문장 라운드 ★ 미팅의 심장

(발표 모드 슬라이드에 제출 문장이 멤버별 카드로 자동 표시. 안 보이면 새로고침.
버디 역할 = 순서 안내 + 마이크 돌리기 + 구체적 칭찬 한 줄. 교정 금지. 끝난 사람이 다음 사람 지목.
★ 타이밍: 1인 2분 목표. 상황 설명 너무 길면 "and the sentence?" 로 부드럽게 넘기기.)

"Main event. Same format as last week. Set the scene, read the sentence out loud, then tell us one part you were unsure about.

I'll tell you the order now."

(★ 순서 미리 발표. Jin·Sunnie·Selina 는 마지막 절반에 배치.)

(진행 루프, 1인 2분:)

1. (버디가 첫 주자만 지명, 이후는 릴레이) "**{Name}**, kick us off."
2. (상황 설명 = 업무 언어가 입 밖으로 나오는 시간. 끊지 말고 듣기)
3. "And the sentence, out loud?"
4. (읽은 뒤) "What was the one part you were least sure about?"
5. (구체적 칭찬 한 줄. 표현보다 장면과의 연결을 짚기)
6. (원하면 동료 버전) "Anyone would say it differently?"
7. "Great. Toss it to whoever's next!"

# Slide 3 · ❌→✅ 드릴 전환

"Let's shift gears. You've just said your own sentence. Now we drill the opposite: I throw you a panic sentence, you give me the calm, professional version.

This is the 위기 변환 드릴. Four rounds. Three sentences each. I read the ❌ version out loud — then you flip it.

Say the whole sentence out loud. If it doesn't come, say 'pass' — we'll come back."

---

# Slide 4 · ❌→✅ 드릴 4라운드 (앱 슬라이드와 흐름 동일)

각 라운드: 버디가 ❌ 문장 읽기 → 멤버가 ✅ 버전으로 소리내어 완성 → ✅ 공개 후 한 번 더 따라 말하기.

---

**🚨 드릴 ① · 긴급 상황 열기**

1. ❌ "Everything is broken!! What do we do??"
   → ✅ "Let's **hop on a call** and triage what's actually failing."

2. ❌ "Drop everything, NOW!!"
   → ✅ "This is a **fire drill**. I need two people on it until noon."

3. ❌ "Whose fault is this?!"
   → ✅ "Blame later. Let's stabilize things for our **client-facing** teams first."

---

**🔍 드릴 ② · 원인 찾기·보고**

4. ❌ "It's probably nothing."
   → ✅ "This latency spike is a **wake-up call**. Let's not ignore it."

5. ❌ "We fixed it!" (실제로는 대충 덮은 것)
   → ✅ "We patched it, but let's not **paper over** the root issue."

6. ❌ "The plan just... failed."
   → ✅ "The rollout **stalled out** at 60 percent. Here's where, and why."

---

**🛡️ 드릴 ③ · 일정 방어**

7. ❌ "Everything is top priority!"
   → ✅ "Security review is the **long pole in the tent**. It goes first."

8. ❌ "Sure, we can add that too..."
   → ✅ "That's **requirements creep**. Let's scope it for phase two."

9. ❌ "They're just lazy."
   → ✅ "Procurement is **slow-walking** us. I'll escalate with a clear deadline."

---

**⚖️ 드릴 ④ · 결단의 언어**

10. ❌ "Let's just keep trying..."
    → ✅ "Two failed pilots. I recommend we **pull the plug**."

11. ❌ "Just trust me on this."
    → ✅ "I have **skin in the game** here. My roadmap depends on this call."

12. ❌ "It was the design team!"
    → ✅ "Let's not **throw anyone under the bus**. The process failed, not a person."

# Slide 6 · 마무리

"Let's close.

(★ Week 3 성장 증거 반드시 짚기.) Three weeks in. Look at where Week 1 was. The words are coming out faster. The hesitation is getting shorter. That's not a coincidence, that's reps.

Week 3 and 4 are the hardest weeks to stay consistent. Not because the content gets harder. Because life doesn't stop. So this week's challenge isn't a word. It's showing up.

One question each, quick round. Not a new goal. Just: **what's one situation this week where something went wrong, and which word from today would have helped?** Real, specific.

(전원 한 바퀴. Jin·Sunnie 는 "짧아도 OK" 미리 말해두기.)

And the four-box note in the app. Especially 'words I couldn't catch.' Whatever goes in there, I'll bring back next week as a two-minute tip.

Next Wednesday night, pick your Week 4 sentence. Deadline Wednesday 23:59."

## 부록 · Week 3 표현 10개 빠른 표 (롤플레이 복습용)

| 표현 | 뜻 | 유의어 |
|---|---|---|
| Fire drill | 갑자기 모두가 달려드는 긴급 소동 | Red alert / Drop everything |
| Hop on a call | 잠깐 통화로 붙다 | Jump on a call / Get on the phone |
| Critical path | 전체 일정을 결정짓는 핵심 경로 | Bottleneck / Blocking item |
| Single point of failure | 하나 무너지면 전체가 서는 지점 | Bottleneck / Key dependency |
| Stalled out | 지연을 넘어 시동이 꺼진 상태 | Blocked / At a standstill |
| Requirements creep | 요구사항이 야금야금 불어나는 현상 | Scope creep / Feature bloat |
| Scramble | 예정 없는 일로 총동원되는 상황 | Rush / All hands on deck |
| Throw under the bus | 책임을 남에게 뒤집어씌우다 | Blame / Pass the buck |
| Skin in the game | 나도 리스크를 짊어지고 있다 | Shared stake / Personal stake |
| Pull the plug | 프로젝트·논의를 완전히 접다 | Shut down / Call it |
