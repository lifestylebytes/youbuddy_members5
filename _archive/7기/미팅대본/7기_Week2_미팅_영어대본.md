# Slide 1 · 오프닝

"Hey everyone, welcome back. Week 2 already.

Before anything, one ground rule again: **this is still the room where you're supposed to get it wrong.** Last week some words didn't come out. That's not a problem to fix, that's data. Tonight's job is the same: say it out loud, even if it's half right.

And one more thing. I'll never cold-call anyone. Before your turn comes, you'll always know. Always.

So, Week 2. Last week was about **reading the field**, before anything becomes official. This week is the other side of that: **The Art of the Argument**. Not arguing to win, but arguing well. How do you push back without being the difficult person in the room? How do you question someone's numbers without making it awkward? How do you stop a meeting from going sideways? That's what Day 6 to 10 is about.

I looked at your sentences from this week. You've been trying to use these words in real situations, and it shows. Tonight we'll pull those sentences into the room.

Plan for tonight: your sentences first, that's the main event. Then a ten-question roleplay to sharpen the reflexes. Then we have a PT presentation. Let's go."

(안전선언 + 발화 환경 재확인. W2 테마 한 줄로 심기. 오늘 순서 미리 안내, 부담감 높은 멤버 안심.)

# Slide 2 · 문장 라운드 ★ 미팅의 심장

(발표 모드 슬라이드에 제출 문장이 멤버별 카드로 자동 표시. 안 보이면 새로고침.
버디 역할 = 순서 안내 + 마이크 돌리기 + 구체적 칭찬 한 줄. 교정 금지. 끝난 사람이 다음 사람 지목.
★ 타이밍: 1인 2분 목표. 상황 설명 너무 길면 "and the sentence?" 로 부드럽게 넘기기.)

"Now the main event. Each of you brought one sentence from your real work this week using one of the Day 6 to 10 words. Here's all you need to do: first, set the scene. What's the situation, who are you talking to? Then read your sentence out loud. That's it. One more thing: after you read it, tell us **one part you were unsure about**. Not because it was wrong, but because noticing that is the skill.

I'll tell you the order now so nobody's caught off guard."

(★ 순서 미리 발표. Jin·Sunnie·Selina 는 마지막 절반에 배치, 앞 사람 보고 긴장 풀릴 시간 줄 것.)

(진행 루프, 1인 2~3분:)

1. (버디가 첫 주자만 지명, 이후는 릴레이) "**{Name}**, kick us off. Set the scene for us."
2. (상황 설명 = 업무 언어가 입 밖으로 나오는 시간. 끊지 말고 듣기)
3. "And the sentence, out loud?"
4. (읽은 뒤) "What was the one part you were least sure about?"
5. (구체적 칭찬 한 줄. 표현 자체보다 장면과 연결한 부분을 짚기)
   예: "I like that you put it in an email context, not just the word itself."
6. (원하면 동료 버전) "Anyone would say it differently in your situation?"
7. "Great. Toss it to whoever's next!"

# Slide 3 · 롤플레이 전환

(★ 전환 멘트: 개인 장면 → 공통 반사신경 훈련. "상황은 달라도 회의 본능은 같다" 연결.)

"Okay, let's shift gears. You've just said your own sentences. Now we train the shared reflexes.

Week 2 is about the moments when meetings get messy. Someone goes off-topic. The numbers look wrong. A plan's about to get approved that nobody's really checked. These are the situations where the right word at the right moment changes everything.

Ten quick scenarios. I'll set the scene, give you the other person's line, and you complete the reply. The key word is blanked out but you get the first letters as a hint. Say the whole sentence out loud, like you're in that meeting. If it doesn't come out, just say 'pass' and we'll come back.

This is not a test. It's reps."

# Slide 4 · 롤플레이 (10문항) · 상황 → 상대 대사 → 내 대답 (첫 글자 힌트)

각 문항: 상황(한국어) 읽기 → 상대방 대사 읽어주기 → 멤버가 대답 문장 전체를 소리내어 완성 → 공개 후 한 번 더.

1. (팀 일정 조율, 10분째 의견이 오락가락함) 🗣️ "We've been going back and forth. Seriously, how do we decide?"
   → 💬 "Let's just do a **quick poll** right now. Drop a thumbs-up for Thursday, thumbs-down for Friday."

2. (발표 중반, 관련 없는 주제가 올라옴) 🗣️ "Actually, since we're all here, should we talk about the Q4 budget too?"
   → 💬 "Good point. Let's **table it** for next week so we can finish today's agenda first."

3. (팀이 상대 제안을 검토 없이 기각하려 함) 🗣️ "Their proposal doesn't make sense. Can we just reject it?"
   → 💬 "Hold on. Let me **steelman** their position first to make sure we're saying no for the right reasons."

4. (새 프로세스 확정 직전) 🗣️ "The plan looks solid. Should we just roll it out?"
   → 💬 "Before we do, can we take five minutes to **poke holes in** it? I'd rather find the gaps now."

5. (보고서 숫자가 전반적으로 좋아 보임) 🗣️ "User numbers look great across the board!"
   → 💬 "Can we **double-click on** the retention rate? The MAU went up but something feels off."

6. (중요한 결정은 안 하고 사소한 것에 20분째) 🗣️ "So... we've been on the slide font for twenty minutes now."
   → 💬 "Yeah, I think we're **bikeshedding**. Can we lock the design and move on to the launch date?"

7. (프로젝트 범위가 계속 불어남) 🗣️ "Let's also add the mobile app, the admin panel, and the API by Q3."
   → 💬 "If we do all of that, we're going to **boil the ocean**. What's the one thing that actually moves the needle?"

8. (경쟁사 리서치를 사흘째 하고 있는 팀원) 🗣️ "I've been comparing competitor pricing for three days and I'm still not done."
   → 💬 "Sounds like you **went down a rabbit hole**. What's the one number you actually need to make the call?"

9. (이미 6개월을 투자한 프로젝트 중단 논의) 🗣️ "We've put six months into this. We can't just stop now."
   → 💬 "Those six months are **sunk cost**. The question is whether the next six months will pay off."

10. (이사회 발표 전날, 자료 준비 완료됨) 🗣️ "The deck looks good. Ready to present to the board tomorrow?"
    → 💬 "Almost. Let's **pressure-test** the revenue assumptions one more time. They always push on those."

> "Ten meetings, ten answers. The ones you passed, those are this week's review list."

(빠르게 패스 모은 것들 한 번 더 돌리기 → 2분.)

Quick reminder of the format: seven minutes of your own work situation, one argument or pushback moment inside the story, and Day 6 to 10 words woven in naturally. Three minutes of Q&A after. The audience's job isn't to judge the English, it's to listen like colleagues.

{발표자 이름}, whenever you're ready."

## 부록 · Week 2 표현 10개 빠른 표 (롤플레이 복습용)

| 표현 | 뜻 | 유의어 |
|---|---|---|
| Quick poll | 간단 투표로 팀 의견 빠르게 모으기 | Straw poll / Show of hands |
| Table it | 안건을 일단 미뤄두다 (나중에 다시) | Parking lot / Put it aside |
| Steelman | 상대 주장 최강 버전으로 세우고 검토하다 | Argue the best version |
| Poke holes in | 허점을 일부러 찾아 찔러보다 | Play devil's advocate |
| Double-click on | 그 지점을 더 깊이 파고들다 | Drill into / Dig deeper into |
| Bikeshedding | 사소한 것에 과몰입해 논쟁하다 | Major on the minor |
| Boil the ocean | 다 하려다 아무것도 못 하다 | Overreach / Try to do everything |
| Go down the rabbit hole | 한 주제에 한없이 빠져들다 | Spiral into detail |
| Sunk cost | 이미 쓴 비용에 발목 잡히기 (매몰비용) | Money already spent |
| Pressure-test | 가정을 일부러 세게 두드려 검증하다 | Stress-test |
